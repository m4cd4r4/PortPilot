The case for the unfinished draft

Every drawer I own holds a half-written letter. I used to call them failures. Lately I think of them as rooms I have not yet decided to furnish.

There is a quiet freedom in leaving the sentence open, in letting a paragraph sit until it knows what it wants to say.
